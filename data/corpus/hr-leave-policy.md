# Annual Leave and Sick Leave Policy

## Annual leave entitlement
Every full-time employee is entitled to 24 working days of paid annual leave (vacation) per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Employees with more than five years of service receive two additional days of annual leave.

## Requesting vacation
Vacation must be requested in the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day of leave. The request is approved by the direct manager. Requests longer than ten consecutive working days also need approval from the department head. Leave during the December code freeze is granted only in exceptional cases.

## Carry-over
Up to five unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used before March 31; after that they expire. Unused leave is not paid out except on termination of employment.

## Sick leave
If you are ill, notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Sick leave of up to three days does not require a medical certificate. For absences longer than three days, upload a doctor's certificate to the HR portal within five working days after returning to work.
