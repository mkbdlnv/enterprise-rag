# Procurement Policy

## Purchase thresholds
Purchases below 1,000 USD can be made by the budget owner with a corporate card. Purchases between 1,000 and 5,000 USD require a purchase order approved by the department head. Purchases above 5,000 USD require at least three competitive quotes and approval from the procurement committee.

## New vendors
Before a new supplier can be paid, it must be registered in the vendor database. Procurement checks the legal registration and bank details of the vendor, and Information Security reviews any vendor that will process company data.

## Software purchases
Software and SaaS subscriptions are purchased only through IT procurement so that licenses are tracked and security reviewed. Do not pay for software with a personal card.
