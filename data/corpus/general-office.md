# Office Facilities and Workplace Booking

## Desk booking
The office uses hot desks. Book a desk or a meeting room in the workplace booking app; bookings can be made up to two weeks in advance. Unclaimed desk bookings are released at 10:30.

## Office hours and access
The office is open from 07:00 to 21:00 on working days. Use your employee badge to enter; a lost badge must be reported to reception, and a replacement costs 10 USD.

## Parking
A limited number of parking places is available and booked in the same app. Electric vehicle chargers are free for employees.

## Guests
Guests must be registered at reception by their host in advance and accompanied at all times.
