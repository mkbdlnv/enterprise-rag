# Laptop and Hardware Requests

## New equipment
New hardware such as a laptop, monitor, docking station or keyboard is requested through the IT service desk portal under "Hardware request". Standard equipment is delivered within five working days. Non-standard equipment requires a business justification and approval from the department head.

## Replacement cycle
Laptops are replaced every three years. Two months before the replacement date the service desk contacts you to schedule the swap. Old devices are wiped and returned to IT; they must not be sold or given away.

## Broken or lost devices
Report a broken device to the service desk; a loaner laptop is issued the same day. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the service desk and to Information Security within one hour so that the device can be locked and wiped remotely.
