# Data Classification and Handling

## Classification levels
All company information belongs to one of four levels. Public: approved for publication, such as marketing materials. Internal: default level for everyday work documents. Confidential: customer data, contracts, financial results before publication. Restricted: personal data of employees, credentials, source code of security components.

## Handling rules
Confidential and restricted information may be shared only with people who need it for their work. Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Confidential documents may not be uploaded to personal cloud storage or public AI tools.

## Using AI assistants
Only the approved internal AI assistant may be used with internal or confidential data. Public chatbots may be used only with public information. Never paste customer personal data or credentials into any AI tool.
