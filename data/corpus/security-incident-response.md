# Security Incident Reporting

## What is an incident
A security incident is any event that may compromise company data or systems: a phishing email you clicked, malware warnings, a lost laptop, suspicious account activity, or data sent to the wrong recipient.

## How to report
Report incidents immediately, and no later than one hour after you notice them, to security@acme.example or by calling the security hotline at extension 911. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook for suspicious emails.

## If you clicked a phishing link
Disconnect the device from the network, do not turn it off, and report the incident to the security team. Change your password from a different device. Do not try to investigate or delete files yourself, because this destroys evidence.

## After reporting
The security team triages the incident within 30 minutes during business hours. You may be asked to hand over the device. Reporting an incident in good faith never leads to disciplinary action.
