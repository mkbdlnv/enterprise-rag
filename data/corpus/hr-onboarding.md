# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before the first day
HR sends the employment contract and a welcome email one week before the start date. IT prepares the laptop and accounts; the login and temporary password are handed over in person on the first day.

## First week checklist
On the first day you meet your manager and your onboarding buddy, an experienced colleague who answers everyday questions during the first three months. During the first week complete the mandatory security awareness training and the data protection course in the learning portal, set up multi-factor authentication, and read the code of conduct.

## Probation period
The probation period lasts three months. At the end of each month you have a feedback meeting with your manager. Remote work becomes available after probation ends.
