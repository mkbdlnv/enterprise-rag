# Business Travel and Expense Policy

## Travel approval
Business trips must be approved by your manager in the travel system before any booking is made. International trips additionally require approval from the department head. Flights and hotels are booked through the corporate travel agency.

## Per diem
Employees receive a daily allowance (per diem) for meals: 40 USD per day for domestic trips and 70 USD per day for international trips. Meals already included in the hotel rate or conference fee are deducted from the per diem.

## Expense reports and receipts
Submit an expense report with scanned receipts in the expense system within 30 days after returning from the trip. Expenses submitted later than 30 days are reimbursed only with approval from the Finance Director. Reimbursement is paid with the next salary.

## Non-reimbursable expenses
Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment and upgrades to business class without prior approval are not reimbursed.
