# Password and Account Security Policy

## Password requirements
Passwords for corporate accounts must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of several unrelated words is recommended. Passwords must not contain your name, login or the company name, and must not be reused from personal accounts.

## Password changes
Passwords do not expire on a schedule. You must change your password immediately if you suspect it was compromised, entered it on a phishing site, or were asked to do so by Information Security.

## Password manager
Use the corporate password manager, Bitwarden, to store work passwords. Sharing passwords by email, chat or sticky notes is forbidden. Shared team credentials are stored in Bitwarden collections managed by team leads.

## Account lockout
After ten failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. To unlock it earlier, use the self-service portal or contact the service desk.
