# VPN Access Guide

## Requesting VPN access
VPN access is requested through the IT service desk portal using the "Remote access" form. Your manager approves the request, after which IT creates the account within one working day. Contractors need an additional approval from the Information Security team.

## Installing the client
Install the GlobalProtect client from the Company Portal on your corporate laptop. Personal devices are not allowed to connect to the corporate VPN. The gateway address is vpn.acme.example.

## Multi-factor authentication
Every VPN login requires multi-factor authentication. Enrol the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone during the first login. If you lose your phone, call the service desk to reset MFA; identity is verified by your manager.

## Troubleshooting
If the VPN does not connect, first check your internet connection and restart the client. Error "Gateway not responding" usually means a hotel or public network blocks the port; switch to a mobile hotspot. If the problem persists, open a ticket with the service desk and attach the client log file.
