# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to three days per week. Roles that require physical presence, such as data center operations and reception, are not eligible. Working from abroad for more than 14 days per year requires written approval from HR and Legal because of tax and data protection rules.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. During core hours remote employees must be reachable in the corporate messenger and attend scheduled meetings with the camera on.

## Equipment and allowance
The company provides a laptop, monitor and headset for remote work. Employees receive a monthly home office allowance of 30 USD to cover internet costs, paid together with the salary. Office chairs can be ordered once every three years through the service desk.

## Security at home
Corporate devices must only be used on a password-protected home network. Public Wi-Fi is allowed only with the VPN switched on. Confidential documents must never be printed at home.
